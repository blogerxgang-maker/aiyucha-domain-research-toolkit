# ICP filing lookup for domain due diligence

ICP备案信息 is useful because it can connect a domain with a historical mainland-China website operator or filing record. It is **not** the same thing as proving current ownership, current website operation, or legal rights to a brand.

## Why buyers check it

For an old domain, filing history can help answer:

- Was the domain previously used for a mainland-facing site?
- Which historical subject or organization appeared in public filing records?
- Does the filing timeline roughly match known website history?
- Did the domain change use or operator over time?

## Interpret carefully

A missing current record does not prove the domain was never filed. Likewise, an old filing record does not prove the old entity still controls the domain today.

The strongest use is as **one historical clue** combined with archived pages, registration history, DNS, public references, and current site evidence.

For domain purchases, record the filing observation with its date and distinguish “current filing” from “historical filing.”
