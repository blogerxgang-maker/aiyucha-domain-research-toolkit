# Baidu index checks and the meaning of “Baidu weight”

“百度权重” is a widely used third-party SEO concept. It is **not an official score published by Baidu**. Different SEO platforms may calculate their own weight estimates from keyword visibility, estimated traffic, or proprietary models.

## Keep two questions separate

1. **Baidu indexing** — are pages from the domain present in Baidu's searchable index?
2. **Third-party Baidu weight** — what does a particular SEO provider estimate about visibility or traffic?

A low or zero third-party weight does not prove a domain has no value, and a non-zero value does not prove the domain is healthy.

For old-domain research, combine Baidu traces with historical pages, backlinks, current accessibility, and topic continuity. Search visibility is one signal among several, especially when data is historical or sparse.
