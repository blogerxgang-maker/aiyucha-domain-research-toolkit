# Domain history and previous website use

Registration age and website history are not the same thing. A domain can be old but unused for long periods, or it can change themes several times while keeping the same registration timeline.

A practical history review asks:

- when public website traces first appear;
- whether snapshots are continuous or separated by long gaps;
- what topics or brands appeared over time;
- whether language, audience, or business model changed materially;
- whether later use introduced obvious spam, doorway, gambling, adult, or unrelated content;
- whether important historical URLs still receive links.

## Continuity matters

For SEO and brand due diligence, a long period of coherent use is usually more informative than the registration year alone. The goal is not to label every change as bad; it is to understand what the domain actually represented on the public web.

Use archived evidence together with backlink history and public citations rather than relying on a single “domain age” number.
