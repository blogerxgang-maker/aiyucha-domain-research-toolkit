# Historical snapshots

Historical snapshots let you inspect what a site actually looked like at different times. They are especially useful when current pages are gone or the domain has changed hands.

## What snapshots can reveal

- old brand and product names;
- topic continuity or abrupt repurposing;
- navigation structure and important historical URLs;
- language and target market;
- redirects or parking periods;
- evidence that a domain was actively used rather than merely registered.

## What a gap means

A missing year does **not** prove the site was offline. Archive coverage is incomplete and varies by site. Treat gaps as missing evidence, not negative evidence.

The useful output is a timeline: years with observed snapshots, major topic changes, and important URLs worth cross-checking against backlink data.
