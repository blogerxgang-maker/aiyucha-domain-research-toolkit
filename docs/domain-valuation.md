# Domain valuation: evidence before a number

A domain price estimate becomes more useful when the evidence is visible. For old or SEO-relevant domains, value can come from several different sources:

- lexical quality and memorability;
- extension and market fit;
- registration and website history;
- surviving brand recognition;
- referring domains and historical links;
- search visibility or old entry points;
- China-market usability such as filing/reachability context;
- legal, brand, spam, or historical-use risks.

## Avoid one-factor valuation

“Old” does not automatically mean valuable. “Many backlinks” does not automatically mean valuable. A short brandable name with little SEO history can still be more valuable than a heavily linked but risky old domain.

A good valuation therefore explains **which evidence supports the estimate**, which signals are missing, and which risks could materially change a buyer's decision.

The number should come after the evidence, not replace it.
