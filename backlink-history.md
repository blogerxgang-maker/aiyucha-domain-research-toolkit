# Historical backlinks

Historical backlink research is useful because today's snapshot can hide what happened during the years when a domain was actively used.

Look for changes in three dimensions:

1. **Volume** — did links grow naturally, collapse, or spike abruptly?
2. **Sources** — did the referring-domain set change from editorial sites to low-quality networks?
3. **Context** — do anchors and linking pages still match the domain's historical topic?

A domain may retain hundreds of thousands of historical links while much of the original value has already disappeared. Conversely, a modest current profile can still reveal durable brand citations from strong independent sites.

Historical links are therefore evidence, not a price tag. Combine them with archived content, current indexability, brand history, and risk signals before valuation.
