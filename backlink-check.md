# Backlink checks: quantity is not the same as quality

A backlink count answers “how many links were observed.” It does not answer “how many independent websites considered this domain worth citing.” That is why **referring domains** often matter more than raw link count.

## Example

A domain can have 800,000 backlinks generated from a small number of templates, sitewide footers, scrapers, or repeated URLs. Another domain may have far fewer links but thousands of genuinely independent referring domains.

Before treating backlink volume as an asset, inspect:

- number of referring domains;
- concentration by domain and page;
- anchor-text distribution;
- topical relevance;
- follow/nofollow and redirect patterns where available;
- historical growth or sudden spikes;
- whether high-value links still resolve.

## A better first question

Instead of “how many backlinks?”, ask **“what kinds of sites linked to this domain, and did that pattern persist?”**

Then combine the answer with [referring-domain analysis](referring-domains.md) and [backlink history](backlink-history.md).
