# Common result-interpretation mistakes

## “Domestic failed, therefore blocked”
Not enough. Check overseas reachability and failure layer first.

## “Both sides failed, therefore definitely blocked”
Usually the opposite: this pattern does not establish a China-specific restriction. Check whether the site exists or the origin is healthy.

## “No archive snapshot, therefore no site existed”
Archive coverage is incomplete. Record it as missing evidence.

## “800k backlinks means strong SEO”
Inspect referring domains, source concentration, anchors, topic relevance, and history.

## “Baidu weight is an official Baidu metric”
It is not. Treat it as a third-party estimate and name the source/method where possible.

## “One DNS answer proves pollution”
No. DNS can vary legitimately. Compare independent networks/resolvers and preserve timestamps.
